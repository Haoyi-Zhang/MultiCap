# Novelty horizon refresh

This release records a fresh Crossref title search over seven query families for 2018--2026. The snapshot is a discovery aid, not proof of global novelty: title search can miss differently named results, and relevance requires reading full papers. Candidates already present in the bibliography are marked. Uncited candidates remain in the snapshot rather than being silently dismissed.

The paper therefore limits its novelty claim to the precise combination it proves: capability-indexed cost semantics, within-model exact materialization bounds, cross-capability separation counterexamples, and matching certificate-carrying compilation paths. It does **not** claim to invent residual functions, nonnegative integer rank, replay evaluation, database fusion, or cache lower bounds.

## Reviewer interpretation

The most defensible contribution is the formal contract that prevents a lower bound from being moved between bit, unbounded-count, and occurrence-record representations. The principal novelty risk is that a reviewer may view the work as a synthesis of known tools. The response is technical rather than rhetorical: every imported ingredient is attributed, every exact theorem states the additional machine assumptions, and the compiler/certificate path is evaluated independently. The snapshot should be refreshed immediately before external submission.
